# Plant-care references

Reviewed October 17, 2026. Guidance is an original, concise English/Tamil paraphrase of these sources. It is curated at development time; no live LLM or paid API is required. Tamil human review is pending. Source institutions have not endorsed this application or its translations.

| Category | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy-pattern care | [University of Minnesota Extension: Growing tomatoes](https://extension.umn.edu/vegetables/growing-tomatoes) | Routine watering, support and observing plant health |
| Early blight | [University of Maryland Extension: Early blight of tomatoes](https://extension.umd.edu/resource/early-blight-tomatoes) | Older/lower-leaf symptoms, rings and yellowing, spacing, mulch and base watering |
| Late blight | [University of Maryland Extension: Late blight of tomato and potato](https://extension.umd.edu/resource/late-blight-tomato-and-potato) | Water-mold explanation, wet-looking lesions, rapid progression, dry foliage and resistant varieties |

General safety framing (expert confirmation, no diagnosis/guaranteed cure or pesticide dosage) is the application's precautionary guidance. Sources describe US horticultural contexts; local prevalence, locally approved chemicals and Tamil Nadu treatment schedules are not inferred. A low-confidence prediction never selects disease-specific care. The separate library remains available as general information.

The measured classifier supports only healthy-pattern, early-blight and late-blight tomato leaves. Symptoms listed by the care library are typical signs to check, not observations made by the classifier in an uploaded photograph.
